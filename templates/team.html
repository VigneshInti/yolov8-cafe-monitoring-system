Project Approach

How the different parts of the system work together.

1

Video Input

The system receives frames from a webcam, uploaded video, or configured video source.

2

Person Detection

YOLOv8 processes the frames and identifies people present in the scene.

3

Tracking

DeepSORT associates detections across frames and maintains tracking identities.

4

Occupancy Calculation

Tracking results are used to determine the current number of people and compare it with the configured capacity.

5

Dashboard

The Flask web application exposes the results through APIs and displays the information in the monitoring dashboard.
